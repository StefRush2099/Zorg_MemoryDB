# Hyperdine X Canonical WWW Links

X-facing Hyperdine article links must use the public canonical `https://www.hyperdine.com/...` host.

Rules for paired Hyperdine/X publishing:

- verify the exact per-article anchor exists in the live Hyperdine landing page HTML;
- normalize the public article URL to `https://www.hyperdine.com/` before posting to X;
- do not post bare `https://hyperdine.com/...` links;
- do not post `hyperdinesystems.com` links;
- do not substitute the top of the feed, a placeholder, a guessed slug, or a truncated anchor;
- if the X post is too long, shorten prose or hashtags first; never remove `www` or truncate the article URL.

This is a publishing correctness rule, not a DNS preflight requirement. For managed Hyperdine publishing, verify the managed page/feed output and exact article anchor, then post the canonical `www.hyperdine.com` public URL.
